06 — Usage Rules

Logo Do's and Don'ts


✓ Do

  • Use the SVG version whenever possible
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "S" in "Settle" on all sides
  • Use logo-dark-bg.svg on navy or dark backgrounds
  • Use the icon-only mark when space is very limited
  • Keep the wordmark and icon mark together unless space requires separation
  • Use the stacked version for square formats like social avatars

✕ Don't

  • Stretch, skew, or distort the logo in any dimension
  • Change any of the brand colors
  • Place the light logo on a medium-gray background — use the dark version
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo
  • Recreate the logo in a different font
  • Place the logo on a busy photo without a solid color backing
  • Resize the icon below 16px — it becomes illegible
